About us


Unmoved is an award winning queer-led production studio dedicated to visual storytelling through to film, photography, writing and research.

We are interested in human stories, queer perspectives, alternative histories and the complicated questions that shape how we see the world.

The Filmmakers

Xan Salinas is a multidisciplinary artist from the South, intersecting identity, queerness, and memory to highlight Black and Chicano narratives. Their practice spans storytelling, documentary production, and creative writing, bridging personal voice with critical inquiry in independent media.

DW McCrary is a queer southern artist and filmmaker working across documentary and experimental narratives. Their work explores the connections between geography, memory, and ancestral inheritance at the intersection of queerness. Their research-based practice uses film to meditate on these temporal and spatial tensions.
