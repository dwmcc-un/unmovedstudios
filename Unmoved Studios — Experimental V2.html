04 / A statement of intent
  

A MANIFESTO FOR THE UNRESOLVED.

  
    

We are not here to provide answers. We are here to ask better questions. To look closer. To sit with discomfort, contradiction and uncertainty.

    

We believe in the independent spirit. In making things with what we have. In experimentation over perfection. In the imperfect, the intimate, the personal and the political.

    

We believe stories can resist. We make space for queer perspectives, alternative histories, cultural memory and voices that have been overlooked, misrepresented or never given the chance to exist.

    

We remain curious. We remain independent. We remain unmoved.
